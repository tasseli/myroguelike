Done (Nov 2026):
- work best prints into messages: hit and death messages go to the bottom UI through game_map.messages (messages are a property of the map)
- creatures spawn on free floor in the generated room; monsters start at least 8 moves from the player
- monsters only attack the player or their own target, others just block them
- every creature has its own letter (gnoll n, uruk u)
- player's health is shown next to the message log
- slow healing for all creatures: 1 hp per 20 turns
- 30 fps cap; faster drawing (only tiles near the player, no square root, cached characters)
- less duplicate code: one Creature __init__ with health_range, Map.add_creature
- smoke test in the repo: python tests/smoke_test.py
- read_moves returns the direction instead of changing the passed position
- death screen shows what killed the player
- the "run right" orc starts wandering when it reaches a wall
- More key (m): a turn's messages are all kept; when they don't fit, rows are numbered and m pages through them
- chasers step around a creature or wall in their way
- press r on the death screen to play again; the game loop is play_game()
- messages from the player's point of view, one line per hit ("The uruk hits you for 3. You have 9 health left.")
- requirements.txt: pygame>=2.5
- w waits a typed number of turns (to heal), shown about 3 turns a second at first, speeding up for long waits; stops early when someone comes next to the player or a key is pressed, or at full health (unless started at full health)
- ? lists the commands for any turn, h explains how things work (both paged with m); a key hint row under the log
- l looks at visible creatures: a blinking cursor, direction keys jump to the next creature that way
- creature tiles colored by mood: red = chasing the player, grey = others
- a grey monster the player hits starts chasing the player ("The golem turns to chase you!")
- yellow flee mood: kobolds left at 2 health or less flee from whoever hurt them until back at full health, then wander
- three more kobolds on the starting map: one hostile, two wandering (4 kobolds in all)
- a grey monster bumping into the player turns red and chases instead of hitting
- l works on the death screen too, e.g. to look at what killed the player
- look: the target is told with distance and direction; t jumps the cursor to it
- green friend mood: a friend stays within 2 steps of its friend, and attacks monsters chasing its friend that come within 2 steps of the friend
- the golem 6 steps from the player starts neutral (wandering) instead of friendly
- c chats: choose whom (no turn), talking takes a turn ("Beep beep, I'm a kobold."); golems offer 5 topics (no turns), loyalty makes the golem a friend
- one mode at a time (game_state["mode"]: PLAY, WAIT_PROMPT, WAITING, LOOK, CHOOSE_CHAT, DIALOGUE, DEAD), each with its own key handler and hint row; a first step toward a game manager
- monsters a friend hits turn on the friend and fight back; a friend defends itself too
- walking into your friend asks "Really attack your friend? (y/n)" (CONFIRM_ATTACK mode)
- golems flee at 2 health or less until half health, then go back to their earlier mood (a fleeing friend still counts as a friend)
- dungeon: two random sized rooms (left and right half) joined by a straight corridor
- pathfinding: chasers and friends take the shortest walkable way (breadth-first search), swapping places to pass others in the corridor
- line of sight: walls block the view; seen tiles are remembered and drawn dim (without creatures)
- p shows the game's earlier messages (history of the last 500: hits, deaths, chat, waiting, dying), newest page first
